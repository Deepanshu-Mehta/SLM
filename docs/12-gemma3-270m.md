# Phase 11: RAFT on Gemma 3 270M (our size, ≈ 750× our pretraining tokens)

**Status: DONE (2026-10-27; ≈ $4.75: GPU $3.86, J1 $0.89).** On the frozen test, Gemma 3 270M (full
fine-tuning, 20K rows) scores **grounded 0.644** (SLM-125M 0.316, the 500M 0.346), hallucination 0.201, recall
0.890, and meets 6 of the 7 targets. The forgetting gate was reported rather than enforced (D-35): bits/byte
+0.308, CaseHOLD −9.6. **Pre-registered reading (≥ 0.60): pretraining scale is the lever even at
≈ 100M transformer parameters.** Option 2's README section was done at the user's request; the website
chapter is built locally but not deployed (the user said to stop after Gemma 3).

Phases 9 and 10 left one comparison open:
- **Phase 10:** same pretraining (≈ 2B tokens), 4× the parameters (125M → 518M): **+0.03** grounded accuracy.
- **Phase 9:** Gemma 2 2B changed both parameters (≈ 20×) and pretraining (≈ 2T tokens): **+0.60**.

**Gemma 3 270M** has about our size and ≈ 6T pretraining tokens:
- 18 layers, hidden 640, 4 heads / 1 KV head;
- ≈ 100M transformer parameters + 168M embedding parameters (262,144 vocabulary);
- SLM-125M has 113M transformer parameters, so the transformer body is about our size.

So it changes the pretraining and holds the size roughly fixed:

> **At about our size, how far does far larger pretraining take grounded QA?**

## 1. Pre-registered reading (fixed now)
Test grounded accuracy (J1, `copy2`) of the 20K model, against SLM-125M's 0.316 and the 500M's 0.346:

| Gemma 3 270M grounded | Reading |
|---|---|
| **≥ 0.60** | Pretraining scale is the lever even at ≈ 100M transformer parameters. A small model can do this task, and it fits our CPU API |
| **0.40 – 0.60** | Pretraining helps a lot, but size still caps reading at this scale |
| **< 0.40** | At ≈ 100M transformer parameters, size caps reading whatever the pretraining |

**Prediction:** 0.45 – 0.60.

## 2. What it is (verified 2026-10-27)
- **Model:** `google/gemma-3-270m`, revision `9b0cfec892e2bc2afd938c98eabe4e4a7b1e0ca1`, Gemma terms (access
  approved).
- **Configuration:**
  - `gemma3_text`, no logit soft-cap;
  - sliding-window attention (512) in 5 of every 6 layers, global attention in the other 3 layers;
  - RoPE 1M global / 10K local;
  - bf16 weights (0.54 GB);
  - needs `transformers` ≥ 4.55.
- **Tokenizer:** the same structure as Gemma 2.
  - ids: pad 0, eos 1, bos 2, unk 3, mask 4, `<start_of_turn>` 105, `<end_of_turn>` 106;
  - ` none` 7293 and ` Passage` 113155 are single tokens; the digit is split (` ` 236743 + digit).
  - `raft_decode.format_for` resolves it to the existing GEMMA format.

## 3. Recipe
- **Data and evaluation:** Phase 9's for Gemma 2.
  - Plain-text format: `<bos>…\nQuestion: …\nSource:` / `… <eos>`.
  - The same 20,340 rows, dev 499, test 1,000.
  - The same J1 judge, τ rule, `copy2`, and `keep="select"`.
  - **No replay** (our corpus is not Gemma's pretraining data, as in Phase 9 §3).
- **Training:**
  - **Full fine-tuning**, not LoRA: at 270M that is cheap (≈ 4.3 GB with Adam), and it is what SLM-125M
    and the 500M got;
  - fp32 master weights + bf16 autocast;
  - AdamW with weight decay 0.1 on matrices (`train.build_optimizer`'s rule), gradient clip 1.0;
  - 3% warmup, cosine to 10%;
  - 52 SFT rows per step, micro-batches by token budget, 3 epochs;
  - logits only at target positions (`gemma_sft`): full logits at 262K vocabulary would take
    ≈ 1 GB per row.
- **The gate:**
  - held-out bits/byte ≤ +0.02 against its own base, tokenizer-free, `<bos>` prefix (as for Gemma 2);
  - CaseHOLD PMI on the same seeded 1,200 items, reported.
- **LR pilot at 5K:** {2e-5, 5e-5} (the 500M's and SLM-125M's full-FT LRs), 4 evals each.
  - **Pre-registered choice:** the higher dev J1 grounded accuracy among gate-passing arms.
  - If neither passes, stop and bring it back.
- **Then 20K** at that LR, 8 evals; τ on dev; **test once**, plain and `copy2`; guards; HF export.
  That matches SLM-125M's and the 500M's 20K runs.

### 11.3 Pilot, full fine-tuning: DONE, gate FAILED (2026-10-27; GPU $0.81 of $2.00; J1 $0.37 incl. closed-book)
**Bits/byte change at eval steps 146 / 291:**

No eval passes the +0.02 gate, so the fallback kept step 146 in both arms.

**Dev J1 (`copy2`) at those checkpoints:**
- LR 2e-5: grounded **0.542**, hallucination 0.259, recall 0.831 (B2 0.58), over-refusal 0.065, citation 0.938;

**Closed-book (base model, J1):**
- 51/500 correct, but 44 of those are Yes/No items (68 in the set).
- **Excluding Yes/No: 7/432 = 1.6%**, so prior knowledge of the test facts is negligible.

**Reading:**
- At about our size, Gemma 3 reads about **twice as well** as SLM-125M on the same 5K rows.
- But full fine-tuning moves its 168M embedding parameters (63% of the model), and its language
  modelling on our text degrades by 18–55%.
- **Pre-registered rule: neither arm passes, so stop and bring it back.**
- **The user chose LoRA (D-34):** keep the gate, as for Gemma 2B (D-31).

- **Size and pretraining compared:**
